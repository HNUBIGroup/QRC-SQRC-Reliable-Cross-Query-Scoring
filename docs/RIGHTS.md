# Rights and source-data scope

This package assembles author-provided experiment outputs and a supplied manuscript. It does not establish or change ownership or licensing of upstream data, model implementations, or the manuscript. No repository-wide open-source or open-data license has been selected on behalf of the authors.

Raw biological source collections, model weights and third-party source repositories are not bundled. Derived score/label tables still carry their upstream provenance; inclusion does not imply that every downstream reuse is unrestricted. Consult the source citations in the manuscript and the preserved audit reports for provenance.

The maintainers should specify their intended code/result-data reuse license after confirming the applicable rights. Until then this notice is informational, not a new license grant.
